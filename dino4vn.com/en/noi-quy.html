Short rules, so everyone actually reads them

We act fast, but never before we have seen the evidence. Nobody has ever been banned just because a lot of people piled on and said they were wrong.

Strictly banned

  • Mesh, going under the map, standing inside rocks to avoid a fight. If you find a terrain bug, report it so it gets fixed; do not use it to win.
  • Cheats, macros, and any software that interferes with the game. Permanent ban on the first offense, no warning, no reduced sentence.
  • Combat logging: quitting the game in the middle of a fight to keep your life. If you lose, you die, then you regrow; that is normal. If you just lost health or are bleeding, the server does not allow safelog (holding H) for 1 minute after your last health loss.
  • Killing yourself to escape a fight. Typing !slay while being chased so the other player cannot eat your corpse is also combat logging. The server does not accept the command for 1 minute after your last health loss, and cancels it if you lose health while waiting; if you try to get around this, an admin will deal with it.
  • Insulting each other's parents, regional discrimination, exposing private lives, sexual harassment. Banned outright, no warning. Arguing about whether Rex or Giga is stronger is fine.
  • Impersonating an admin. Naming yourself or using an avatar like staff, or messaging “I am an admin, you are banned” to scare people: ban.
  • Buying or selling accounts, selling spots on the server, selling dinosaurs or items for real money. This server does not charge anyone for priority, and does not let anyone else do it either.
  • Camping the area where new players appear to kill them over and over. Hunting each other in the middle of the map is gameplay; sitting at the spawn point is not.
  • Spamming Discord, posting links to other servers, repeatedly tagging admins.

Juvenile protection zones (“coconut forest”)

The island has 7 protection zones for juveniles; see where they are on the Map page. Big dinosaurs going in there to hunt juveniles is the fastest way to make new players leave the server, so the server handles this itself instead of waiting for an admin to be online.

  • Dinosaurs above 45% of adult growth may not stay in a protection zone. You can still pass through: you get 1 minute 30 seconds from the moment you step in, and the server sends you a countdown in the chat box, right when you enter and more often toward the end.
  • If you are still inside when that time is up, you lose 1% health every second until you leave the zone. If you stay, you die and lose the dinosaur, and nobody can restore it.
  • Leaving and re-entering right away does not reset the 1 minute 30 seconds. The counter only returns to 0 after you have been outside the zone for 5 minutes in a row. Each time you re-enter you get 5 seconds to run through.
  • Troodon and Herrerasaurus are exempt. All other species are treated the same.

If a bigger dinosaur chases you into one, just keep running out: 40 seconds is more than enough, and the one chasing you is under exactly the same rule.

Not a violation

  • Killing you without saying anything first. This is The Isle; any dinosaur can die at any moment. Being killed unfairly is not a reason to open a report.

How to report

  • You need a clip or a screenshot. A spoken account alone is not enough to act on, and it easily turns into wrongly accusing someone.
  • Post it in the report channel on Discord. Do not message admins privately: nobody can cross-check a private message, and it easily gets lost.
  • Include: roughly what time it was, which species you were playing, and which species and name the reported player had. If any of that is missing, we have to ask again, which slows things down for both sides.

Storing a dinosaur in the garage

  • You can only store once you have been out of combat for 1 minute. If you just lost health or are bleeding, you must wait 1 minute after your last health loss; health lost to hunger or thirst counts too. If you lose health while waiting, or move more than 3 meters from where you pressed, it is canceled and the dinosaur stays as it was. Storing in the middle of a fight turns the garage into an escape button, and wastes the effort of whoever is chasing you.
  • A dinosaur you store is a dinosaur you put away. You cannot store it and keep playing it at the same time.
  • To take one out, you must be playing a dinosaur of the same species. Pick that species, spawn in, and only then can you take out the stored one. While you are on the species selection screen you cannot take it out yet.
  • Taking out also means standing still until the wait bar finishes (the command tells you the number of seconds; it is currently 1 minute), and people within 300 meters are warned in advance that something is about to appear. If you lose health during that time, or move more than 3 meters from where you were when the warning went out, it is canceled and the dinosaur stays safe in the garage. Your position is checked every second, so you really must stand still; if you go somewhere else, the warning given to the people around you would point to the wrong place. The garage is a place to store dinosaurs, not a way to appear behind other people.
  • Each player has 3 slots. If all 3 are full, you must take one out before storing a new one.
  • You cannot store over a slot that already holds a dinosaur. Whatever a slot holds is safe there; to reuse the slot, take the old one out first.
  • You can store both in the game and on the web. In the game, type !gui (goes into the first empty slot) or !gui <slot number>, and take out with !lay <slot number>; on the web, open the Garage page after signing in with Steam.

Self-kill with !slay

  • Use it to change species without running around looking for a place to die. In the game, type exactly !slay, with one exclamation mark, not /slay; on the web, open the My page after signing in with Steam.
  • You must stand still for 30 seconds. Losing health or moving more than 3 meters while waiting cancels it and the dinosaur stays alive. If you just lost health or are bleeding, you can only type it 1 minute later. That is deliberate, so nobody uses this command to run away from a fight.
  • Changed your mind? Type !huy while the countdown is running.
  • A dinosaur that is self-killed is gone for good. The garage does not keep it, and admins do not restore a dinosaur you killed yourself. If you want to keep it to play later, use !gui, not this command.

Dinosaur lost to a server fault

  • Report it on Discord as soon as you can. Say the species, the growth level, and what time you lost it. Those three things are what admins need to find your dinosaur.
  • Admins review each case. There is no button you can press yourself: restoring a dinosaur is an admin decision, not an automatic action.
  • Dying while playing is not restored. Hunted, starved, drowned, fell: that is the game. Only cases where the dinosaur was lost because of a server fault are considered.
  • If you used !slay yourself, it is not restored. You were asked and you agreed.
  • To be sure you keep a dinosaur, use !gui. The garage is the only way you can protect it yourself; everything else depends on an admin reviewing it.

How we handle cases

  • We look at the evidence before deciding. We do not rule on cases that involve us; we bring in an outside person to look at it with us.
  • If you were punished and think it was wrong, open a ticket on Discord within 7 days, with your own evidence. We read it again. If we were wrong, we lift the punishment and say clearly that we did.

The full rules are on Discord
