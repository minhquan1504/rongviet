How the score works

Three parts, added together:

Survival time 1.5 points per minute survived
Distance measuring
Adult dinosaurs Bonus points for a dino raised to 100%, counting at most 1 per day

Where these numbers come from

The server records itself every few dozen seconds: who is playing, which dino, and where. The leaderboard adds that up and is rebuilt every few minutes.

Why the numbers can be off

  • This is an estimate, not a stopwatch. Any time the recording system is interrupted, that stretch is not counted.
  • A dino that becomes an adult and dies within a few dozen seconds may not be counted in time.
  • Switching to another dino (dying, or taking a different one out of the garage) restarts the distance for that dino.

The error can only go low, never high. We would rather undercount than give points for something that did not happen.

Want a spot on the board?

There are no tricks, just play. But these three things help the most:

  1. Survive longer

    About half of the score comes from time survived. Knowing when to run is worth more than knowing how to fight.

  2. Raise a dino to adulthood

    Each day, one dino raised to 100% earns bonus points. A second one on the same day adds nothing.

  3. Keep moving

    Distance earns points. Anyone who migrates often or forages far has an edge.
