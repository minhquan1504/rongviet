How to join the Rong Viet server

It takes about three minutes to read. If you are already in, skip straight to the voice section.

On this page

  1. Four steps
  2. Common traps
  3. Turn on voice chat
  4. Store your dinosaur in the garage
  5. FAQ

Four steps

  1. Own the game on Steam

    The Isle is sold on Steam. There is no free version and no demo. You have to buy it to play, on any server, not just this one.

  2. Switch to the evrima branch

    This is the step people miss most often.

    Open Steam ▸ Library ▸ right-click The Isle ▸ Properties ▸ the Betas tab ▸ in the Beta Participation box choose evrima ▸ close the window and wait for Steam to finish downloading.

    The game has two branches running side by side: Legacy (the old version) and Evrima (the one everybody plays now). By default Steam keeps you on the old branch. On the old branch, Rong Viet and almost every other server will not show up.

    Sign you are still on the wrong branch: the server browser is empty or shows only a few servers you have never heard of.

  3. Find the server in the game

    Open the game ▸ Server Browser ▸ choose the Unofficial tab ▸ type Rong Viet in the search box.

    Pick the entry [VN] Rong Viet and click it.

  4. Pick a species and play

    No password, no approval, no role to get first. Pick a species and play.

Common traps

  • Typing the full name finds nothing. Type only Rong Viet. Do not type the [SEA/VN] brackets, and do not leave a stray space at the start of the search box.
  • Wrong tab. The server browser does not open on the Unofficial tab by default. All community servers are on that tab.
  • The list has not finished loading. Click Refresh and wait a few seconds. The first time you open the browser after starting the game is usually slow.
  • Someone gives you an IP address to paste. It will not work. The Isle does not allow connecting by IP; every way in goes through the in-game server browser. Anyone telling you to paste an IP somewhere is either mistaken or trying to scam you.
  • The server is full. Just click it and the game puts you in a queue. Leave the waiting screen as it is and you will go straight in when it is your turn. If you quit and come back, you lose your place and start from the back.
  • Still cannot get in. Ask on Discord and include: whether you switched to the evrima branch, which tab you are on, what you typed in the search box, and what error the screen shows. Those four details are enough for us to work out the problem in a single reply.

Turn on voice chat

The Rong Viet server has in-game voice chat: you hear players who are near you, and stop hearing them when you move away.

The base game does not have this feature. To hear others you need to install one extra thing, once.

  1. Download the launcher

    The Rong Viet Launcher gets direction and distance right, and lets you choose your microphone and headphones inside the app.

    Download Rong Viet Launcher

    This is the only launcher that works on Rong Viet. Voice launchers from other servers cannot hear anyone here.

  2. When it is installed, open the launcher

  3. Find [VN] Rong Viet in the list and click it

    Done. Enter the game and you can hear people.

A few small notes

  • Open the launcher before you enter the game, and leave it running in the background for the whole session.
  • You do not need to install Mumble. If you are still running old Mumble, close it.
  • The first time Windows asks for microphone permission, choose Allow.
  • If the game shows “account is not linked”, type this in the chat box: /link-account <the code shown in the launcher>

Still cannot hear anyone? Ask on Discord and include: whether the launcher is open, whether you clicked the server name, and whether you can hear other people or only you are affected.

Store your dinosaur in the garage

The garage keeps your dinosaur safe while you are not playing, and you can take it out any time. Use it on the web at the garage page, or type commands right in the game.

Do it in the game

!guiStore your current dinosaur in the first empty slot
!gui 2Store it in slot 2
!lay 1Take the dinosaur in slot 1 out

What to know before you store and take out

  • If you just lost health or are bleeding, you can store only after 1 minute. Health lost to hunger or thirst counts too. The command tells you how many seconds are left. !slay and safelog (holding H) follow the same rule.
  • After you press it, stand still until the wait bar finishes (the number of seconds shows on screen when you press; it is currently 1 minute). If you lose health during that time, the system cancels automatically and your dinosaur is safe, nothing is lost. This is how we stop people from storing a dinosaur in the middle of a fight so the other side cannot take the kill. Health lost to hunger or thirst also counts, so feed and water your dinosaur before storing. Moving more than 3 meters from where you were standing when you pressed also cancels it. Your position is checked every second, so really stand still.
  • The dinosaur must be grown enough (at least 40% of adult).
  • Once stored, that dinosaur leaves the world and you go back to the species selection screen. It stays safe in the garage and you can take it out any time.
  • To take one out, you must be PLAYING a dinosaur of the same species. Pick that species on the selection screen, spawn in, then type !lay: the stored dinosaur's data is applied onto the one you just spawned. While you are on the species selection screen you cannot take it out yet.
  • Taking out also needs you to stand still for 1 minute. Losing health while waiting cancels it, and moving more than 3 meters from where you were standing when you pressed also cancels it. Your position is checked every second, so stand still until the dinosaur appears. If it is canceled, your dinosaur is still safe in the garage and you lose nothing.
  • Players around you get a warning. As soon as you press take out, anyone within 300 meters gets a message that something is about to appear. The garage is not a place to pop up behind other people by surprise.

Using the garage to escape a fight breaks the rules, exactly like quitting the game mid-fight. See the rules.

FAQ

I cannot find Rong Viet in the server browser?

Check four things in this order: have you switched to the evrima branch, are you on the Unofficial tab, did you type exactly Rong Viet in the search box, and did you click Refresh. Nine times out of ten the problem is the first one.

Can I join the server by IP?

No. The Isle does not support connecting by IP; everyone has to use the in-game server browser.

I clicked the server and got put in a queue. What should I do?

Nothing. When the server is full the game queues you automatically. Leave the waiting screen as it is and you go straight in when it is your turn. If you quit and come back you lose your place and start from the back.

The Eggs tab keeps spinning on “Connecting to server…”?

This is not an error. That tab only shows content when the server has a nest open to the public. For a nest to exist, two adult dinosaurs of the same species and opposite sexes have to pair up, build a nest, lay and finish incubating, and then the nest owner opens it to others. When no nest is open, the game leaves the waiting screen as it is and says nothing.

Just pick a species from the other tabs and play normally.

Does installing voice cost anything?

No. Download it and it works.

Is there a virus?

No. This is an app made by Rong Viet itself, downloaded straight from the Rong Viet website. Windows will warn you once because the file has not been signed with a purchased certificate; click More info → Run anyway and you are done.

Can I join the server without installing voice?

Yes. You just will not hear anyone speak.

I can hear one person but not another?

The other person has not installed it, or installed it but has not clicked the server name in the launcher.

The volume goes up and down?

That is normal. Rong Viet voice gets quieter as the other person moves away, and it depends on which direction they are in; go too far and you stop hearing them completely.
