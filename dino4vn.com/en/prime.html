What is Prime Elder

Grow a dino to old age and it becomes an Elder. But there are two very different kinds of Elder: Prime is stronger, Frail is weaker. What decides it is how many conditions you meet before the dino reaches 75% growth. After that mark it is too late.

  2. The ten conditions
  3. Four conditions where you have to go somewhere

Three things to know first

  • There is no switch to turn on. Prime is a built-in game mechanic, not something the server configures. The only way is to make it achievable.
  • Conditions count only before the 75% mark. After that, doing more doesn't count.
  • You can see your real progress. The ten rows below show which ones you have met on My page, updated live while you play.

The ten conditions

You need 5 of the 10 before the 75% mark. Five small species get condition 10 for free, so they only have to do 4 of the others.

1. Sanctuary Visit a Sanctuary while you are still a juvenile. The easiest of the ten: do it early, don't wait until you have grown.
2. Hatched from a nest Be hatched by a pair of players, not spawned by yourself. depends on the community
3. Perfect Diet Reach Perfect Diet: eat all three nutrient groups, at least 1% of each.
4. Mass Migration Visit Rong Viet's Mass Migration zone. Entering once is enough. This zone is always migrating, so there is no event to wait for. Open the map and you can see where it is.
5. Two Migration zones Visit two different Migration zones. Migration zones are almost always active, so there is no need to watch the clock. Going back to the same zone does not count twice.
6. Four Patrol zones Visit four different Patrol zones out of 61. Each zone counts once, but unlike condition 5, Patrol zones do not need to be active. Many zones sit close together, so one loop is enough.
7. Not Infertile Never get the Infertile mutation in your whole life. Passive, you just need to avoid bad luck.
8. No Muscle Spasms Never get Muscle Spasms in your whole life. Also passive.
9. Reach 95% growth Turns on by itself when your dino reaches 95% growth. But the condition table is locked in at the 75% mark, so this line never counts toward the total. Don't include it in your plans. too late to count for Prime
10. Right species Be one of: Hypsilophodon, Troodon, Beipiaosaurus, Dryosaurus or Deinosuchus. This one is free if you are playing the right species.

Four conditions where you have to go somewhere

Conditions 1, 4, 5 and 6 only count when you actually enter the zone. The zone list is taken straight from the game's map file, so this is all of them, not just some.

1. Sanctuary Visit 1 of the 7 zones, while still a juvenile. See on the map
4. Mass Migration You only need to enter 1 time. This zone is always migrating, so there is no event to watch for. Progress is counted automatically: as soon as you enter, the server messages you, and box number 4 lights up on your page and on the in-game overlay. See on the map
5. Migration You need 2 different zones, out of 12. Migration zones are almost always active, so there is no need to watch the clock. Going back to an old zone does not count twice. Progress is counted automatically: each time you visit a new zone, the server tells you how many are still missing, and that number also shows on your page and on the in-game overlay. See on the map
6. Patrol You need 4 different zones, out of 61. Many zones sit close together, so one loop is enough. Progress is counted automatically: each time you visit a new zone, the server tells you how many are still missing, and that number also shows on your page and on the in-game overlay. See on the map

The Mass Migration zone and the Migration zones are two different layers on the map. After entering the Mass Migration zone you still have to visit two Migration zones for condition 5.

Why it's harder than you think

Dinos on this server grow faster than the game default. Growing faster means the window before the 75% mark gets shorter, while the migration and patrol zones still follow real-world clock time.

In other words: fast growth gets you an adult sooner, but makes the conditions that need you to travel to many places harder. That is a trade-off, not a bug.

Tip: the four passive conditions (1, 3, 7, 8) come almost free if you remember to visit a Sanctuary while small and eat all three nutrient groups. Those three plus one zone condition reach the threshold.

Rebirth (Entomb)

Grow to 100% and you become an Elder. Then lie down to rest, open the status panel and choose ENTOMB: the dino ends its own life to be reborn as a juvenile of the same species, keeping its mutations and getting stronger.

This is also a built-in game mechanic, not something the server turns on or off.
